    One workspace

    Less hunting.
    More doing.

    The Ledger turns scattered operational knowledge into a clear, searchable system your team can use in the moment, and keeps everyone connected through messages.

    Protocols

    Know the next step.

    Follow timed steps with actions, supplies and warnings. Switch between Simple and Full views, and tick off actions as you go.

    Safety

    Make safer decisions.

    Mark an allergy to flag on protocols, and every product containing that ingredient gets a red badge right where you work.

    Products

    Keep product knowledge close.

    Browse products and their ingredient lists, keep an eye on back stock, and send recommendations to the front desk when a treatment ends.

    Agenda

    See the day clearly.

    Open today’s appointments from Home, and stamp a client’s arrival so the team hears about it right away.

    Notes

    Document without friction.

    Capture SOAP notes while the session is fresh and turn a completed session into a useful, portable record.

    Messages

    Keep everyone aligned.

    Every message shows Sent, Delivered or Read. Managers can step in, and staff see when they do.

    Everything connects.

    Messages run through the whole app. An arrival on the agenda reaches the therapist. The last step of a protocol can send product recommendations to the front desk. A note lives beside the session it came from. A shift swap or a low-stock flag travels as a message too.

    • ✓Sent, Delivered and Read on every message
    • ✓Search the whole workspace in one place
    • ✓Give each role the right view, from therapist to manager

    Staff messages are visible to management, and the app says so: “This is not a private messaging platform.”
